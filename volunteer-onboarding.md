## About Everything Open Source

Everything Open Source is a non-profit community dedicated to making 
open-source contributions accessible to developers, designers, technical 
writers, and learners across Africa and beyond.

We run open-source sprints, mentorship programs, community events, and 
initiatives like Books for Techies to lower the barriers to participation 
in open source. We are community-driven, volunteer-powered, and deeply 
committed to making sure more people get to experience the joy and growth 
that comes from contributing to open source.

- 🌍 Website: [everythingopensource.my.canva.site](https://everythingopensource.my.canva.site)

## How We Communicate

**Day-to-day communication** happens on Discord. This is where the 
team checks in, shares updates, asks questions, and stays connected. 
Please check Discord regularly and respond to messages within 48 hours 
where possible.

**Formal updates and documentation** live on GitHub. Important 
decisions, program updates, and community resources are documented 
here so they are accessible to everyone.

**Email** is used for external communications including sponsor outreach, 
maintainer coordination, and partner correspondence.

**Team check-ins** happen *(add cadence, e.g. bi-weekly)* and are a 
space to share progress, raise blockers, and stay aligned as a team. 
Attendance is encouraged for all volunteers.

**A few communication norms we ask everyone to follow:**

- Be responsive. If you cannot complete something on time, let the 
  team know early rather than going quiet.
- Be kind. Written communication can lose nuance. Assume good intent 
  and ask for clarification before assuming the worst.
- Be transparent. If something is not working or you are struggling, 
  say so. We would rather know early and problem-solve together.
- Be present. Show up to check-ins and team conversations where 
  possible. Volunteering works best when the team feels connected.

## How We Work

**We work asynchronously.** The Everything Open Source volunteer team 
is distributed, which means we do not all work at the same time. 
Async-first means documenting decisions clearly, communicating progress 
proactively, and not expecting instant responses.

**We document everything.** If you make a decision, run a process, or 
build something, write it down somewhere the team can find it. Good 
documentation is one of the most valuable things you can contribute.

**We work in sprints and cycles.** Some of our work is event-driven 
and intense around sprint periods. Other work is ongoing and steady. 
Plan your time accordingly and communicate your availability clearly.

**We give and receive feedback openly.** Feedback is how we grow, 
both as individuals and as an organization. Share it kindly, receive 
it gracefully, and always focus on the work rather than the person.

**We celebrate contributions.** No contribution is too small. We 
recognize and appreciate the work of every volunteer and community 
member, and we want you to feel valued for everything you bring.

## Your First Week

Here is a simple plan for getting started:

**Day 1 — Get set up**
- Read this onboarding guide in full
- Read the Code of Conduct
- Join Discord and introduce yourself in the introductions channel
- Make sure you have access to all the tools you need for your role
- Review your role description and note any questions

**Day 2 to 3 — Get oriented**
- Explore the Everything Open Source GitHub organization and 
  familiarize yourself with the repositories
- Review any existing documentation relevant to your role
- Reach out to team members whose work overlaps with yours and 
  introduce yourself
- Book your onboarding call with your team lead if you have not 
  already done so

**Day 4 to 5 — Get started**
- Attend your onboarding call
- Identify your first task or contribution and get started
- Ask questions freely — there are no silly questions here
- Share a short update in Discord letting the team know how your 
  first week is going

> You do not need to have everything figured out in week one. Take 
your time, ask questions, and know that the team is here to support you.

## Community Guidelines

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How much time do I need to commit each week?**
It depends on your role. Most volunteer roles ask for between 5 and 10 hours per week, with heavier periods around sprint cycles and 
campaign launches. Your role description will give you a clearer picture of what to expect.

**What if I cannot make a commitment one week?**
Life happens. Just let the team know as early as possible so we can 
plan around it. We are a volunteer-powered organization and we 
understand that flexibility is part of how we work.

**Will I be recognized for my contributions?**
Absolutely. We celebrate our volunteers across our social media 
channels, in our community updates, and in our contributor spotlights. 
Your work will not go unnoticed.

**Can I contribute to areas outside my role?**
Yes, as long as your primary responsibilities are covered. We 
encourage cross-team collaboration and love when volunteers bring 
ideas and energy to areas beyond their core focus.

**What if I want to leave the volunteer team?**
We completely understand that circumstances change. If you need to 
step back, please give us as much notice as possible so we can 
transition your responsibilities smoothly. We will always be grateful 
for what you contributed.

**Who do I talk to if I have a problem or concern?**
Reach out to the core leadership team directly via 
everythingopensource@gmail.com. All concerns are taken seriously and 
handled with care.
